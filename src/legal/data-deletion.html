The current Heyawake Daily app does not create a game account or upload your puzzle progress to a developer-operated server. Your board states, points, XP, achievements, theme ownership, streaks, and settings are stored locally.

Delete all local game data

  1. Open Android Settings and find Apps.
  2. Select Heyawake Daily.
  3. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  4. Choose Clear storage or Clear data and confirm.

Menu names vary by device. Clearing only the cache does not delete saved progress. Uninstalling the app can also remove its local data.

Deleted local progress cannot be recovered without a previously exported backup. There is no developer-operated cloud account from which we can restore points, completed levels, or themes. If you want to preserve progress, save a manual backup in the app’s Settings before clearing storage. The app currently has no paid purchases.

Disable reminders without deleting progress

Turn Daily reminders off in the app's Settings screen, or disable notifications for Heyawake Daily in Android Settings. This does not remove completed levels, points, or themes.

Browser demo

The demo does not save a board to browser storage. Reloading or closing its page clears its in-memory state.

Exported files and beta counts

Turning off Local beta measurements in Settings deletes local event counts. Exported progress backups and beta reports remain where you saved them. Delete those files separately from your chosen folder or cloud provider. Clearing app storage does not delete exported files.
