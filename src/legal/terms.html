Puzzles and progress

The current app includes 10,000 uniquely solvable 4 × 4 classic puzzles, 300 larger 5 × 5 and 6 × 6 journey puzzles, and a separate 300-puzzle daily collection. Daily challenges follow your device's local date and repeat after the daily collection has been visited. Difficulty labels are estimates that may be refined with player feedback. Your daily streak and goals follow the device calendar.

Boards and progression are stored on your device. No game account or cloud synchronization is included in this build. Clearing app storage, uninstalling, losing the device, or changing devices can cause loss of progress. You can manually export a progress backup through Android's file picker to a folder or cloud provider you choose. Restore requires a valid backup file and replaces the progress on the destination device. Export a backup before deleting local data if you want to preserve it.

Points, XP, hints, and themes

Points and XP are virtual gameplay items. They have no monetary value, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be transferred to other players. The app shows the reward rules and the point cost of a hint or theme before you spend. One-time solve rewards, daily rewards, goal rewards, and badge rewards are not paid repeatedly for the same qualifying event.

The current build does not sell points or include in-app purchases. Earned points do not expire. A theme unlocked with points remains owned in your local progress. Free teaching hints explain logical deductions. An optional paid reveal marks one correct cell and consumes the displayed number of points. Optional rewarded ads offer 250 virtual points only when Google reports that the reward was earned. No reward is promised for unavailable, failed, or unfinished ads. Ads need internet, are subject to age and privacy settings, and are never required to access any puzzle. Development builds use test ads; live release serving remains disabled until configured by the publisher.
