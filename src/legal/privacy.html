Information stored by the app

The app stores your puzzle boards, completed levels, points, XP, achievements, theme purchases, hint history, daily challenge dates, goals, streak freezes, monthly collections, tutorial progress, solving times, reminder preferences, an optional advertising age group, and rewarded-ad receipts in the app's local storage. This is used to provide gameplay and preserve your progress. It is not uploaded to an Onvexa Labs server.

Version 0.7.0 includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for optional rewarded ads and advertising privacy choices. It does not include a separate gameplay analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, account system, or cloud synchronization. It does not request your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or photos.

Backups you choose to save

You can export a progress backup through Android's file picker to a local folder or a cloud provider you select. Backups contain saved progress, puzzle boards, tutorial state, and gameplay settings. They are not encrypted by the game. The destination provider handles the file under its own privacy policy. Restoring a backup replaces local progress after confirmation. We do not receive backup files. Delete exported files separately from the destination when you no longer want them.

Optional local beta measurements

Local beta event counts are off by default. If enabled, the app counts app opens, tutorial steps, puzzle starts and completions, and hint requests on your device. Turning the option off deletes those event counts. You may export a report containing these counts, tutorial completion, completed-puzzle counts, personal best times, and whether local play history shows a return after 1, 7, or 30 days. No report is uploaded automatically, and no advertising identifier or account is attached. A recipient receives the report only if you separately choose to send it.

Optional notifications

Evening reminders are off by default. If you enable them, Android may ask for notification permission. The app schedules reminders locally and checks whether you have finished the day's challenge. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings. Reminder preferences and delivery dates stay on your device.

Sharing and contacting support

The app’s Send feedback feature prepares an email draft containing your message and, if you leave the optional details enabled, the app version, current screen or puzzle, Android version, and device manufacturer and model. You can review these details, omit them, or copy the draft. The game does not send the email automatically and does not attach an advertising identifier or your saved boards.

If you tap Share after a solve, Android opens its share sheet. You choose the recipient and service. The shared text can include the puzzle level or date, your completed-puzzle count, and your daily streak. The chosen service's privacy policy applies to what you share there.

If you contact us by email, we receive the address, message, and any files you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request, and keep them only as needed to handle the request, follow up, or meet legal requirements. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information.

Website hosting and browser demo

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading it sends technical request information, including your IP address, to the hosting provider. GitHub may process this information for delivery and security under its privacy statement. This website does not add analytics trackers, advertising pixels, marketing cookies, or a contact form. Fonts and artwork are hosted with the site.

The playable browser demo keeps its board state in the page's memory. It does not create an account, set cookies, upload your moves, or save the demo board after a page reload.

Optional rewarded ads and advertising privacy

The full puzzle game, interactive tutorial, daily challenges, and saved progress work without internet and without watching ads. There are no banners, automatic interstitials, or app-open ads. Players may explicitly open the optional ad feature to earn 250 virtual points after Google confirms that a reward has been earned. Closing an ad early or an ad failing does not grant a reward.

A neutral age-group screen appears before using optional ads. The app is intended for people aged 13 and older. Players selecting 13–15 use the full game without ads. Players selecting 16 or older can choose the online ad feature. The selected group is stored locally, is not included in exported progress backups, and does not include a birthday. No ad is loaded merely by opening a puzzle or starting the app.

For eligible players choosing the ad feature, UMP checks applicable consent requirements and displays Google's configured privacy message when required. A privacy-service error prevents an ad request and does not restrict gameplay. Where required, advertising privacy choices can be reopened from Settings. Choosing not to consent does not restrict access to any puzzle; Google may make limited ads available according to the applicable privacy choices.

Once the online advertising service is used, Google may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, ad and app interactions, performance diagnostics, and device or app identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. The app removes the advertising-ID permission on Android versions where it is required; this does not mean all identifiers or advertising-related data are absent. Google describes its SDK processing in its Mobile Ads data disclosure and privacy policy. Google states SDK data is encrypted in transit. Once initialized, the SDK may perform background processing for that session; choosing not to load further ads does not uninstall or reset it.

Development builds use Google's test ad unit, never the live unit. Live serving is disabled by default in release builds until the publisher completes the AdMob message and release setup. Test advertising and consent requests may still contact Google. Offline play never requires these online services. The app records rewarded-ad receipts locally to avoid awarding the same reward twice.

Legal bases and your choices

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, responding to a support request relies on handling your request or our legitimate interest in providing support. Website delivery and security rely on the legitimate interest in operating a secure site. Optional app reminders are enabled by your choice and, where required, Android permission. Advertising privacy choices are handled through Google UMP where applicable; only eligible players who choose the ad feature initiate that flow.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, withdraw consent where it applies, or receive portable personal data. You may also complain to the competent data-protection authority. Contact the publisher above to exercise applicable rights. We cannot retrieve app progress that has never left your device.

Deleting data and retention

Local game data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Device settings and platform behavior may affect retention. See Your data for instructions. We do not operate a central game account to delete. Hosting-provider retention follows its own policy.

Children and updates

The game is intended for ages 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The optional ad flow uses the age groups described above. If a child sends personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations. The Play Store target-audience declaration must match the released app.
