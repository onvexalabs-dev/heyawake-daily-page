Quick answers

  • Tap a cell to cycle empty → shaded → white. Every cell must be marked to complete the puzzle.
  • Daily puzzles use their own collection, separate from the classic and larger journey puzzles.
  • A first solve pays once per level. A daily bonus pays once per daily date.
  • Teaching hints are free; revealing a cell costs 50 points. Theme purchases use earned points and stay owned locally.
  • Clearing storage or uninstalling can erase local progress. Save a manual backup in Settings before deleting data. A valid exported backup can be restored on another device; automatic cloud sync is not included.
  • Reminders are optional. Android battery settings can affect their delivery.

Privacy requests

Read our privacy policy and data deletion instructions. We cannot see or recover app progress that has never left your device.
